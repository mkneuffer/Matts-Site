Impact of AI in VR

Process write-up: VR visualization of the impact of AI, The Global Lab at WPI

Project overview

Introduction

The goal of this VR experience was to help users understand AI's workings, focusing on environmental impacts like water usage, electricity consumption, and emissions. This project aimed to educate users and provide actionable insights into mitigating these impacts.

Initial Planning and Requirement Analysis

  • Engaged stakeholders to define objectives, incorporating feedback from WPI stakeholders, supervisors, and public sentiment.
  • Analyzed knowledge gaps and aligned with WPI Global Lab’s educational goals.
  • Defined project scope focusing on environmental visualization, AI functionality, and user engagement.

Tools & Technologies

  • Hardware: Meta Quest 3 selected for its standalone VR application support and versatility.
  • Software: Unity chosen for rapid prototyping and OpenXR SDK for cross-platform compatibility.
  • Visualization Techniques: Best practices in VR visualization to make abstract concepts tangible and actionable.

Task Breakdown

  • Stages: Research, visualization design, iterative feature development.
  • Milestones: Key demos tied to stakeholder feedback and feature testing.

Progress Tracking and Milestones

  • Maintained progress spreadsheet with tasks, deadlines, and progress bars.
  • Delivered regular demos for supervisor feedback and guided iterative improvements.

Data and Asset Management

  • Collaborated with partners to source data from research papers and reports.
  • Organized assets in shared drives and used tools like Blender and ZBrush for 3D modeling.

Implementation and Technical Details

  • Overcame challenges in scaling VR environments for large datasets.
  • Implemented optimizations using prefabs and reusable scripts for modular development.
  • Enhanced performance for smooth, high-quality VR experiences.

Quality Assurance and Testing

  • Iterative testing with internal and external users.
  • Functional testing conducted at key milestones.
  • Feedback loops informed improvements and ensured objectives were met.

Obstacle Management and Change Handling

  • Addressed challenges in translating complex AI systems into interactive elements.
  • Adapted to changes in SDK and hardware requirements as needed.

Retrospective and Lessons Learned

  • Aligned technology with user needs for effective education.
  • Valued stakeholder engagement and iterative feedback.
  • Learned optimization strategies for VR training simulations.

Conclusion

This project showcased the importance of aligning technology with user needs, emphasizing feedback-driven iterations to achieve educational goals effectively in immersive media.
